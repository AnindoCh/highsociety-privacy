High Society Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: High Society (Android package com.a14thdesignstudio.highsociety) · Developer: 14th Design

High Society is a learning app with short lessons about art, history, wildlife and more. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

The short version

  • Your progress lives on your device. There are no accounts and we run no servers of our own.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after a lesson, and optional reward videos you choose to watch. Google may process some device data to deliver them, as described below.
  • Analytics is optional. You are asked first, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Reminders (Hatch's Notes) are local notifications scheduled on your phone.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps its own data in private storage on your phone: lessons you have finished, your scores and in-app progress (such as Prestige, Standing and Stamps), the time of day you usually play (used to time reminders) and your settings (sound, colours, haptics and so on). We cannot see this data. It is not uploaded or shared. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, removes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

High Society uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. To deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or completed) and diagnostic data.

This processing is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown, and you can change them later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics (Unity Analytics, optional)

The app asks whether you are happy to share how you play. Only if you agree, it sends usage data to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies) so we can improve the lessons: for example which screens and lessons are opened and finished, session length, device model, OS and app version, an app-generated installation ID, and approximate country derived from your IP address. You can stop this at any time in Settings → Privacy → Analytics. Unity processes this data under the Unity Privacy Policy.

Notifications

After your first finished lesson, the app may ask Android for permission to post notifications. If you allow it, Hatch's Notes are scheduled on your device as gentle reminders. No notification data leaves your phone. You can turn them off in Settings → Hatch's Notes or in Android's notification settings.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load ads, send optional analytics and open links you tap.
  • Notifications: for the optional reminders.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or files.

Children

High Society is a general-audience app for ages 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Apart from Google AdMob and, if you opt in, Unity Analytics as described above, the app does not send data to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on our own servers. Data on your device is removed when you reset progress or uninstall the app. To ask us to delete data, email connect@buzzedmenstudio.com with the subject "High Society data deletion"; we reply within 30 days. For data processed by Google or Unity, use their controls linked above, and we will help you with those too.

Security

Your progress stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google and Unity services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
